About Us

A host at heart, for over 25 years.

Jed’s Party Services is owned and operated by Jed Kessler, a Fair Lawn native who has spent his career in hospitality.

Jed Kessler

Owner / Operator

Jed Kessler has been in the food service industry for over 25 years. At an early age Jed loved to host and entertain his friends and family. When he was fifteen years old he entered the service industry, starting at local eateries and moving on to some of the finest dining restaurants in Bergen County, New Jersey.

He lives in his hometown of Fair Lawn, New Jersey with his wife, kids and two dogs.

To make others happy, and to always exceed expectations.

Jed’s personal philosophy
