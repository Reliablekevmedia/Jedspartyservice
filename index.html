Our Mission

How It Works

From first call to last call

  1. Free consultation

    Call, email or send an event request. Phone and email consultations are free; we learn your party’s needs before pricing and scheduling are set.

  2. Reserve your date

    A 25% non-refundable deposit holds your event date. The 75% balance is due within 72 hours of your event.

  3. Enjoy your party

    We arrive to set up, serve your guests with polish and a smile, then clean up, so the end of the night is as easy as the start.
